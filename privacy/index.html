Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Agog has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no server, and your countdowns are never sent to one.

Your countdowns

Countdowns are stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. The developer can't read them. If you turn iCloud off, they stay on the device.

Photos

A photo you choose as a background is resized and kept on your device, where the app and its widgets can show it. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Shared countdowns

When you share a countdown, Apple's iCloud sharing makes it visible to the people you invite. They can see its title, date and style, and the names iCloud shows for each participant. You can stop sharing or leave a shared countdown at any time.

Purchases

Agog Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never sees your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat to check whether you have Plus. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app and your App Store purchase history for Agog. It does not receive your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Widgets and Live Activities read a file stored on your device.

Children

Agog doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
